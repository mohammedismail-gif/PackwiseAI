# PackWise AI - prototype (SIH 2026, PS SIH26236)

Run: open index.html in a browser (internet needed once for the font and QR library).

- index.html - page structure
- style.css - styling, light and dark themes
- script.js - data and recommendation engine

In script.js:
- F = food profiles (respiration rate, baseline shelf life, barrier limits)
- P = packaging options (OTR, WVTR, cost, eco score, strength)
- need() = required OTR/WVTR, evaluate() = scoring and shelf life, risks() = risk levels

The numbers are illustrative demo values. Replace them with data from your references.
